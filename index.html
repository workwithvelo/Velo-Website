Questions we get asked

If something's not covered here, ask us directly. We answer these ourselves, not through a call centre.

Is £399 really the full price?

Yes. £399 covers the full design and build of your site. The only other cost is the £49/month plan that keeps it hosted, backed up and maintained once it's live.

Can I cancel the £49/month plan?

Yes, any time. There's no fixed contract. If you cancel, your site can be moved to hosting of your own choosing.

How long does it take to go live?

Most sites are live within a week or two of our first call, depending on how quickly we get your logo, photos and any details we need from you.

Do you write the content for me?

Yes. We write the pages based on what you tell us about your work, your area and your customers. You review everything before it goes live.

I already have a website. Can you replace it?

Yes, this is common. We'll point your domain to the new site once it's approved, so there's no downtime for your business.

What happens if I need changes later?

Small text and image updates are included in the £49/month plan. Bigger changes, like new pages or a redesign, are quoted separately.
